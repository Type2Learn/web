Type2Learn Active Learning

Learn by typing. Build knowledge that stays.

Type2Learn is an education initiative being developed with a nonprofit mission. It uses typing as an active-learning method—not merely as typing practice. Learners recall, explain, correct, apply, and revisit academic ideas to build durable understanding.

Type2Learn was founded by Muhammad Taha Bin Zaeem. The founding team also includes Muhammad Hamiz Bin Kashif, Co-founder and Engineering Lead; Muhammad Fahad Younus, Co-founder and AI Lead; Idrees Babar, Co-founder and Research Lead; and Alizay Hassan, Co-founder and Product Lead.

Neurodivergent co-design is being prepared

Type2Learn is being designed to involve neurodivergent learners—including people with dyslexia, ADHD, and autism—together with educators, families, accessibility specialists, special-education professionals, learning professionals, and learning-science researchers through structured co-design and testing.

Current status: the participation model is in preparation. Type2Learn does not yet claim completed external co-design findings, clinical validation, endorsement, or formal advisory relationships.

Active development, educational purpose, independent identity

Some public experiences are prototypes or planned concepts and are labelled accordingly. Type2Learn does not diagnose, treat, or clinically assess any condition. Type2Learn Active Learning is independent and is not affiliated with Type to Learn or similarly named browser extensions.
