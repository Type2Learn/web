Type2Learn Active Learning

Learn by typing. Build knowledge that stays.

Type2Learn is an active-learning platform developed with a nonprofit mission. A learner meets one bounded idea, retrieves it in their own words, improves it with specific feedback, applies it in a meaningful situation, and returns later. Typing makes thinking visible; it is never treated as a speed test.

A learning engine, learner-controlled support, and reviewed course publishing

Courses use a visible loop of reading or listening, recall, complete-section typing, checks, application, and return. Learners can choose presentation controls such as layout, text, spacing, read-aloud, background noise, and bounded “I’m stuck” help. Educational source material follows a reviewed path from submission to structured course record, testable lesson package, and human-approved publication.

Early participation is public. Formal co-design claims remain separate.

The public record documents input from 1 professional, 3 teachers, 5 learner contributors, and 25 product reviews. These early contributions have informed product decisions about accessible flow, sensory controls, clear steps, and support language.

Current status: Type2Learn does not claim that this early record is a completed formal co-design study, clinical validation, endorsement, or a formal advisory relationship. Structured co-design and outcome testing require their own consent, method, safeguards, and published limitations.

Active development, educational purpose, independent identity

Some public experiences are prototypes or planned concepts and are labelled accordingly. Type2Learn does not diagnose, treat, or clinically assess any condition. Type2Learn Active Learning is independent and is not affiliated with Type to Learn or similarly named browser extensions.
