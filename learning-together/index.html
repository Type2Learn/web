Type2Learn · Learning together

One learning experience. Clear responsibilities around it.

Type2Learn is being designed for learners, families, educators, and schools. Learners keep control of their supports and work; adults receive only the information needed to support meaningful learning.

For learners

See a clear next action, choose helpful controls, and return to saved work without shame.

For families, educators, and schools

Support learning without surveillance. School use remains subject to documented safeguards, accessibility review, permissions, and meaningful educational purpose.
